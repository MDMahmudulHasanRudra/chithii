# Cookies

The website at getassist.pro sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. That is why there is no cookie banner.

On this page

## This website (getassist.pro)
- No cookies of any kind.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
- No fonts, scripts or images loaded from other companies’ servers.

## The Get Assist app
The app you sign in to, at demo.getassist.pro <https://demo.getassist.pro/login>, is a separate service. The app uses only the cookies it needs to sign you in and keep you signed in: a session cookie, a security (CSRF) cookie and a cookie that remembers where to return after signing in. It uses no analytics or advertising cookies.
