What Get Assist does

One inbox for your messages

Messages from your Facebook Page and Instagram professional account arrive in one place, so nothing is answered twice or missed.

Answers from your own knowledge

The AI replies using the information you give it, such as delivery times, policies and product details.

Your team takes over when needed

When a question needs a person, the conversation is handed to someone on your team with the full history.

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Connect your channels

    Connect your Facebook Page or Instagram professional account. You choose which accounts to connect.

  2. Step 2

    Add your knowledge

    Add the answers and policies your customers ask about most. This is what the AI works from.

  3. Step 3

    AI drafts or sends answers

    Decide whether the AI sends replies itself or drafts them for your team to check first.

  4. Step 4

    Hand over to your team

    Conversations that need a person move to your team, with the earlier messages still in view.

Questions

Which messaging channels does Get Assist work with?

Facebook Messenger and Instagram Direct Messages. Both integrations are being prepared and will be offered to eligible customers when the necessary platform access and testing requirements are complete.

What kind of Instagram account do I need?

An Instagram professional account (business or creator). Personal accounts cannot be connected.

Does the AI reply without anyone checking?

Only if you set it up that way. The AI can draft answers for your team to review, and any conversation can be handed to a person.

How do I disconnect a Page or Instagram account?

You can disconnect it inside Get Assist, and you can also remove access from your own Facebook or Instagram settings. The Messenger and Instagram pages explain both.

How do I ask for my data to be deleted?

Follow the steps on our data deletion page. It also explains what happens if you messaged a business that uses Get Assist.

How much does Get Assist cost?

Pricing has not been published yet. Contact us and we will tell you what applies to your business.
